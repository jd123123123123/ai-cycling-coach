## Decisions Made

1. **Intensity thresholds: TSS/h of 45/75/110 plus absolute TSS>=120 'big' override.** The plan suggested these numbers; I kept them. The absolute-TSS override exists because a 5-hour Z2 day at ~25 TSS/h is still a 'big' day physiologically even though the per-hour rate is easy.

2. **v1 ships narrative only on the total series; per_sport stays null.** Reason: users hover the total chart. Per-sport narratives would triple the narrator work with no UX signal for v1.

3. **Compute narratives on every /pmc call, don't cache.** Narratives are cheap pure-function calls over O(days) entries. Caching would introduce language-aware invalidation complexity for zero wall-clock benefit.

4. **Ban-list enforced in tests, not at runtime.** Runtime jargon scrubbing (like 05.3's scrubber) defends against LLM output. Here the templates are hand-written and deterministic — the invariant belongs in tests so the contract is enforced at CI time, not at serve time.

5. **uPlot tooltip via cursor-idx bridge, not canvas paint.** Keeps the tooltip stylable with CSS vars, a11y-friendlier, and responsive without hand-coding text metrics on the canvas.
