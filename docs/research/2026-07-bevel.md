# Bevel (bevel.health) — Competitive Research Report

*Compiled July 2026 (web research: App Store review RSS, Bevel's public feedback board, TechCrunch, third-party reviews). Reddit was crawler-blocked; community sentiment is secondhand.*

## 1. Overview

**What it is:** Bevel is a software-only "connected health coach" iOS app that reads data your wearable already collects (via Apple Health) and re-interprets it into WHOOP-style daily scores — **Recovery, Sleep, Strain, Stress** — plus nutrition, strength training, Biological Age, Health Records, and a conversational AI coach ("Bevel Intelligence"). Positioning: "The Everything Health App" / "the Whoop experience without the strap or hardware subscription."

**Company:** Founded 2023 (originally "Superset") by Grey Nguyen, Ben Yang, Aditya Agarwal, out of South Park Commons. $4M seed, then **$10M Series A led by General Catalyst (Oct 2025)**. Metrics they publicize: **100K+ DAU, 8x growth in a year, users open the app ~8x/day, 90-day retention >80%**, "over 1 million members." App Store: **4.8★, ~13–28.6K ratings, #20 Health & Fitness**. (Sources: https://techcrunch.com/2025/10/30/bevel-raises-10m-series-a-from-general-catalyst-for-its-ai-health-companion/, https://www.bevel.health/, https://apps.apple.com/us/app/bevel-ai-health-coach/id6456176249)

**Data sources:** Apple Watch (primary, deepest — complications + full watchOS app), Oura, Garmin, Amazfit, WHOOP/Fitbit via Apple Health, Google Health, CGMs (Dexcom, Libre), and **50+ lab providers** (Labcorp, Quest, LifeLabs, Medicover) for bloodwork.

**Pricing (moved around a lot — a story in itself):**
- Originally paid; **v2.4.0 (Dec 2025) made the core app entirely free** — Recovery/Sleep/Strain/Stress scores, nutrition logging, Sleep Alarm, Strength Builder, full history. Public commitment: "features free today stay free." (https://gadgetsandwearables.com/2025/12/21/bevel-app-free/)
- **Bevel Pro: $14.99/mo or $99.99/yr** (as of Bevel 3.0; earlier ~$5.99–9.99/mo — prices rose in 2026). Pro unlocks: Bevel Intelligence AI coaching, Health Records, Biological Age. Annual subscribers grandfathered.
- **AI usage is metered:** Pro includes a weekly allowance of Bevel Intelligence usage; extra "Bevel Intelligence Credits" sold at **$4.99–$49.99** packs.

**Target user:** Apple Watch owners who want WHOOP/Oura-grade interpretation without $300+ hardware + subscription. Marketing personas: **Commander** (goal-driven optimizer), **Friend** (casual tracker), **Guardian** (long-term health), **Data Nerd** (metrics enthusiast) — which double as selectable AI coaching personalities.

## 2. Feature Inventory

| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Recovery** | % score from resting HRV + RHR; "ready to tackle the day or time to slow down." ~2-week baseline learning period. |
| **Strain** | Personalized daily exertion %, target strain recommendation, color-coded low/normal/high history graph. Reviewers: "The strain score encourages workouts far more than my Apple Watch rings." |
| **Sleep** | Stage breakdown, personalized sleep need, sleep debt/balance, **Smart Alarm** ("wakes you at the perfect moment in your sleep cycle"). |
| **Stress** | Separate daily stress metric + trigger identification, linked to journal habits. |
| **Energy Bank** | Signature metric: cumulative body-battery-style energy curve **combining Recovery + Sleep + Strain + Stress into one metric** through the day. Watch complication. |
| **Nutrition Score** | Meal logging via barcode/photo/text-AI; macro AND micronutrient breakdown; glucose/CGM integration; single Nutrition Score (Bevel 2.0, Feb 2025). |
| **Strength Builder** | 700+ exercise library, templates, real-time Watch logging ("Live Sync"), **muscle-group spiderweb graph** of training balance. Called better than WHOOP's strength tracking. |
| **Cardio Load / Training Status** | Cardio load balancing; states: detraining / maintaining / productive / peaking / fatigued / overtraining. |
| **Biological Age** (Pro) | Weekly "one score that shows how you're aging" from sleep, activity, fitness, RHR, lifestyle + blood biomarkers. |
| **Health Records** (Pro) | Upload/parse lab results; 50+ lab integrations; feeds the AI's context. |
| **Bevel Intelligence** (Pro) | Conversational AI over all your data: Q&A ("I'm feeling not rested today. What happened?"), proactive check-ins, daily summaries, nudges, AI food logging, AI-generated training plans, citations to research (sciencedirect, ncbi, plos). |
| **Journal / Timeline** | Habit journaling (caffeine, water, screen time, alcohol…) correlated against scores; day timeline. |
| **Misc** | Cycle tracking, blood pressure, light/dark themes with illustrative or minimal backgrounds, Watch app + complications. |

## 3. UI/UX Design Analysis

- **Structure:** Home dashboard with core scores as **circular gauges/rings** (Strain, Recovery, Sleep) at top, then a card stack: "stress and energy" card (Energy Bank curve), vitals, nutrition, journal prompts. Separate sections for Fitness (calendar views, cardio load, training status), Nutrition, Journal/Timeline, Intelligence chat. Detail screens are trend-graph heavy with color-coded ranges.
- **Design language:** Consistently described as **"remarkably Apple-esque," "clean, minimal, highly responsive,"** "intentional" navigation, "seamless transitions," "intuitive color coding and smooth animations" — explicitly avoiding "the cluttered, data-heavy look of professional athletic software." "Less like a spreadsheet of medical data and more like a refined, intuitive dashboard." (https://neura.health/insight/bevel-health-app-in-depth-review)
- **Personalization of look:** light/dark themes, illustrative vs minimalist backgrounds; "dark mode is gorgeous."
- **Interaction philosophy:** built for a **"ten-second morning ritual"** glance, not deep exploration — "low friction… compound value through trend analysis over weeks." (https://www.autonomous.ai/ourblog/bevel-app-review)
- **Copy tone:** conversational, encouraging, non-clinical but specific: "Start the day with confidence," "Ready When You Are," "Know when to push, when to pause, and when you're ready to perform," "Push With Purpose," "Bevel meets you where you are."
- App Store reviewers repeatedly cite the UI itself as a reason to stay: "The UI is beautiful and very intuitive"; "the colors and charts make all of the info super easy to consume"; "Every feature seems to be thought out so well before release."

## 4. Review Sentiment

### Loved (recurring themes)
- **Consolidation/value:** replaces Whoop + a food app + a gym log; "The free version can replace several other apps." No hardware lock-in.
- **Behavior change from scores:** "gone from monthly runs to near daily" (strain score); "warned me about overtraining… my HRV is trending up and my VO2."
- **Generous free tier:** "I love that they don't add limits to the free features."
- **AI when it lands:** "First 'personal coach' that can fully account for medical variables AND adjust recommendations accordingly" (retired MD reviewer); "you can tell it exactly how to communicate (I recommend the 'data nerd' concise personality)."
- **Design + responsive dev team:** "Dev team is awesome."

### Criticized / frustrating
- **Deceptive "free" advertising** — the loudest 1-star theme: "LIARS! NOT FREE!!!", "the dude literally says he doesn't believe in charging people… but it's $19 a month for full use," "you can't even move forward without signing up for a free trial." Ad claims vs. paywall reality is a serious trust wound.
- **AI reliability:** "Great App, Terrible AI… it hallucinates every single conversation… needs to sit and process questions for minutes"; AI forgets context ("I tell it I'm on a rest day. Later that night it tells me I can still get that exercise in"); repetitive default advice ("the default answer to low recovery is take it easy and take a walk. I will get nowhere if I follow that all the time"); notifications that deep-link to nowhere.
- **Black-box scoring:** "presents confident conclusions without transparent reasoning… users cannot identify which inputs drove the number." (autonomous.ai)
- **Routine dependency:** scores degrade to vague guidance during travel/irregular schedules.
- **Breadth over depth:** matches no specialist tool (Hevy for lifting, MFP for nutrition) in depth; strength credit calculations sometimes wrong ("3% upper back credit despite numerous pull-ups"); no MyFitnessPal import.
- **Ecosystem bias:** Garmin/Oura/Coros integration "feels secondary"; sync issues; app must be opened to sync.
- **Price sensitivity:** $14.99/mo felt high after the price rise.

### Missing (top of their own feedback board, https://feedback.bevel.health/feature-requests)
Supplement/vitamin tracking (53 votes), background sync, **recovery-time estimate after training**, a yearly **"Bevel Wrapped"** summary, richer workout HR analysis, steps timeline, naps in sleep need, iPad version, Android (none), no percentile/"vs others" comparisons anywhere.

## 5. AI / Insight Generation Specifics

- **Four selectable coaching personalities** that restyle ALL copy in the app, not just chat: **Data Nerd** (analytical, "the why behind your numbers"), **Guardian** (calm, long-term framing), **Friend** (warm, motivating), **Commander** (direct: "Now remember you committed to going to bed, phone down, sleep. That's an order."). Users can add free-text tone guidance on top. Arguably their most differentiated UX idea.
- Insights are **data-grounded and citable** (links to sciencedirect/ncbi/plos), phrased as answers to natural questions ("I'm feeling not rested today. What happened?").
- **Proactive**: check-ins, daily summaries, congratulation notifications, streak awareness (buggy: falsely broke a "beer-free streak").
- **Adaptive**: "adapts recommendations based on how individual users respond to stress, movement, and dietary choices" — learns response patterns, not just thresholds. (TechCrunch)
- **Metered**: AI actions consume weekly credits; standard non-AI features never do.

## 6. Lessons for our AI cycling coach app

1. **Personality selector for the coach voice** — Data Nerd/Friend/Commander/Guardian tones applied across every surface (not just chat) is cheap on top of our recommendation engine and heavily praised. Cyclists split naturally into "give me watts and CIs" vs "encourage me" personas.
2. **One fused daily-energy metric (Energy Bank)** — a single intraday curve is their most-referenced card. Analogue for us: a fused readiness/W′bal-style "energy today" curve on the Today page.
3. **Explain the score or lose trust** — the #1 substantive criticism is black-box scoring. Always show "which inputs drove today's number."
4. **Close the loop from score → prescription** — Bevel gives a strain target but "doesn't recommend specific workouts to hit that strain." We already prescribe workouts; explicitly linking "readiness X → therefore today's session is Y (or swap to Z)" beats Bevel at its weakest point.
5. **Degrade honestly** — Bevel needs 2 weeks of baseline and says so; reviewers respected the honesty. Matches our "honest —" principle.
6. **Free-core / paid-AI split works** — free scores drove 8 opens/day and 80% 90-day retention; only AI, records, Biological Age are paid. But **never let marketing say "free" if onboarding forces a trial** — that mismatch is Bevel's entire 1-star column.
7. **AI memory/consistency is table stakes** — hallucinated streaks, forgotten rest days, repetitive "take a walk" advice turn 5-star users into 3-star ones. Constrain AI output with deterministic state (planned rest days, logged events) rather than free generation.
8. **"Wrapped" yearly recap and recovery-time estimates** are top user-demanded features Bevel hasn't shipped — both natural for a cycling app (season recap; TSB-based "recovered by Thursday" estimates).
9. **Design bar:** circular score gauges, color-coded range bands, spiderweb balance charts, gorgeous dark mode, 10-second-glance dashboard — reviewers explicitly retain the app because the UI is beautiful.
10. **Citations in insights** — grounding recommendations in linked research raises perceived credibility.
