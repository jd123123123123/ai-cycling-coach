## Decisions Made

- **Decimal avoidance in sentence text:** `f"CTL eased back {abs(delta):.1f}"` would produce "1.0" which split('.') treats as two sentences. Fixed by rounding to integer in PMC technical sentence — no precision loss since CTL deltas are meaningful at whole-number granularity.
- **Separation of TID (sentence 3) and recovery framing (sentence 5):** Initially merged recovery framing into _tid_sentence early-return, causing redundancy. Split so sentence 3 describes the actual intensity distribution (mostly easy on recovery week) and sentence 5 gives the positive "your body absorbs the work" message.
- **Event sentence suppressed beyond 112 days:** Too far out to anchor week-level coaching copy. Aligns with Friel's base1 phase where the event is known but irrelevant to this week's prescription.
