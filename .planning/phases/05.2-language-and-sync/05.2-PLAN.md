<objective>
Two asks, one plan:

**A) Language toggle** — every piece of coach text supports two modes. Default is **simple** (plain-English, fun, for people who don't live in watts and TSS). Opt-in is **technical** (the current Coggan/Seiler vocabulary with inline explanations so a beginner can still follow). Toggle lives in the masthead, persists per-browser.

**B) Sync button** — a visible "Sync Garmin now" button in the masthead that forces a Garmin sync and invalidates all related caches so the dashboard refreshes with fresh activities + daily metrics.

The backend already has `POST /garmin/sync` and `POST /coach/brief/refresh` — this plan wires both + adds language routing.
</objective>

<context>
**User bar (verbatim):** "make all text easy understandable for even the non fitness freaks and implement a toggle for language so people can decide if they wanna have a very clear easy to understand sentences that are fun to read or deeply technical including explanations. Also implement a sync button to force sync"

**Examples of the tone difference:**

Simple (default):
- Title: "Easy spin today"
- Rationale: "You had a chill weekend, so let's start the week with an easy hour on the bike. No pressure — just keep it comfortable."
- Workout: "About 60 minutes at a pace where you can still chat"
- Alternate: "Got 30 min? Just spin the legs"
- Why-tired: "Your sleep's been light and you're still catching up from last week's rides"

Technical (with inline tooltips/explanations):
- Title: "Z2 endurance — 60min @ 65% FTP (~163W)"
- Rationale: "TSB +5, yesterday's TSS was low (18). Base2 phase calls for aerobic volume — 65% FTP holds cardiac drift under 5% per San Millán."
- Workout: Full interval blocks with watts, RPE, zone numbers
- Alternate: "recovery_30 · 30min Z1 · 15 TSS"
- Why-tired: "HRV 7d mean 43ms vs 28d 49ms (−12%); cumulative sleep debt 4.3h; Z2% = 24% (grey-zone creep vs Seiler's 20% ceiling)"

Both modes must serve the same underlying data — simple doesn't mean less accurate, just less jargon.
</context>
