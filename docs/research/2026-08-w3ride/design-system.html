Typography

HARD · Work Sans, embedded

Work Sans Regular / Medium / SemiBold — no Bold. Body defaults to 18px Medium with a global letter-spacing:-0.94px on every text node. 06

Five moves worth stealing

Separable from the crypto economy — these are pure craft.

Inverted surface depth

Cards (#0F0F0F) are darker than the page (#252525), with zero shadow. Panels read as cut-outs, and lime pops harder against deeper black than it would against grey.

One accent that never dims

#B5FF23 is the accent in light theme, dark theme, the splash screen, and the launcher background. The app boots as a full lime screen.

Two accents = two states

Violet means pending / promised; lime means earned / settled. The same pill flips between them on settle — and every art asset ships in both.

A track is its own fill at 30% alpha

One mechanical rule, four colour variants, always harmonious, no separate "track grey" token to maintain.

The button is the timer

Slide-to-claim's knob refills over 24h, so the primary action, its disabled state, and the countdown are a single object rather than a button plus a label.

07

What isn't real

EYE-only — not in the shipped bundle

The circular "Next milestone" rings and the "Badges / SEE ALL" rail from the marketing screenshots have zero copy-string matches in v0.14.6 (and "Silver Tier" isn't a string either). They're marketing renders — documented from the image with rebuild notes, never cited as shipped behaviour. Ironically they're the two surfaces closest to what a coaching app would want, so they get an honest asterisk.
